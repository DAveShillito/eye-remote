Eye Remote

The one rule

Only one eye switched on near the gamepad. It remembers just one eye at a time, and pairing it with the other one makes it refuse this one. Slide switch on the side pushed forward.

If it isn't working

  1. Light flashing, never stopsCheck the eye is powered. Give it 10 s. Then switch the gamepad off and on.
  2. Still flashing: re-pair itSwitch the gamepad off. Hold Start for more than 8 s until the blue light flashes. Let go and wait about 10 s: the eye pairs with it by itself.
  3. Light off, but the stick does nothingWrong mode. Check the slide switch is forward. Switch off, then switch on holding Start + Circle together (game mode).
  4. None of that workedUse this app: Connect, pick Eye1. It has the same stick and buttons. Gyro mode needs no controller at all.
  5. Eye frozen or acting oddlyUnplug the power bank cable and plug it back in. The eye starts in Random mode with your settings.
